VAT Calculator: Support

Need help or found a wrong tax rate? Email naorcho@gmail.com and include your country (and state or province).

Common questions

How do I change the country or tax rate? Tap the country under the title, or open Settings. Under Tax Rate you can also enter a custom rate.

Why does the US rate look low? The app uses the state rate. Cities and counties add their own tax; enter your total local rate as a custom rate.

How do I clear the number? Long-press the delete key.
