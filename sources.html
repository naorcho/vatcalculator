VAT Calculator: Sources

VAT Calculator is not a government app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or acting for any government or tax authority. Tax rates are for guidance and may change; check with your local tax authority. Found a wrong rate? Email naorcho@gmail.com.

The tax rates in the app come from these official sources. For EU countries, see also the European Commission: VAT rates in EU countries.
