VAT Calculator: Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

VAT Calculator ("the app") is made by Naor Melamed. This policy explains what data the app handles.

What stays on your device

Your settings (country, tax rate, language, number format, theme) are stored only on your device. We have no servers and no accounts, and we never see the amounts you type.

Advertising

The app is free and shows banner ads from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising identifier, approximate location derived from your IP address, information about how you interact with ads, and diagnostic data. Google's use of this data is described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

  • In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before any personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in the app: Settings → Privacy Settings.
  • On iPhone, the app asks for permission before tracking (App Tracking Transparency). If you decline, ads are not personalised using your advertising identifier.
  • On Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.
